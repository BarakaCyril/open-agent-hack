# Data

## Source

`sample/kamis_dry_maize.csv` was already in this repo. It is a KAMIS (Kenya Agricultural Market Information System) style price extract described as exported on 14 October 2026. The public source URL was not recorded in the repo, and `download_data.py` is still the original stub. Do not treat the file as a fresh pull from KAMIS.

`sample/records_sample.json` (0 records) and `sample/metadata.csv` (header only) were already here and are unused by the agents.

## Licence or usage note

KAMIS terms of use are unconfirmed. This subset is for the hackathon demo only. Do not assume a redistribution licence.

Weather, storage-loss rules, news, and the farmer ledger are synthetic. Each of those files is marked `synthetic: true` (or `synthetic=true` on every CSV row). They are not KMD forecasts, gazette notices, or real farm records.

## Subset

Counties: Kirinyaga (Ngurubani Market), Nakuru (Wakahiu), Uasin Gishu (Eldoret), Machakos, Nairobi (Wakulima), Garissa, Nyandarua (Ol Kalou).

## How to regenerate

The sample KAMIS file is not regenerated by the stub script, and the refresh scripts do not write into `data/sample/`. Weather, rules, news, and the ledger in that folder are hand-authored synthetic inputs.

`counties.csv` holds administrative centroids for the counties in the price sample. Each row is an OpenStreetMap Nominatim relation (`source` and `source_url`). Do not replace a row with a town geocoder hit.

`npx tsx scripts/refresh_kamis.ts` downloads the KAMIS market export (Dry Maize, Export to Excel) and upserts `$ZETARIS_MAIZE_DIR/kamis_dry_maize.csv`, which `maize.kamis_prices` already reads. `npx tsx scripts/refresh_weather.ts <county>` downloads the Open-Meteo daily forecast for that county's coordinates and upserts `weather.json` for `maize.weather_daily`. Requests wait 2 seconds, and raw bodies go to `data/cache/` (gitignored). KAMIS terms are unconfirmed. Open-Meteo data is CC BY 4.0 (Weather data by Open-Meteo.com). Nominatim coordinates are ODbL 1.0.

## Limitations

- One to twelve quotes per county is not a national price index.
- Wholesale KES per 90 kg bag is the series the tools use. Retail and volume are carried but not modelled.
- The newest KAMIS dates in the file are 18 October 2026, while the export note says 14 October 2026. Later-dated rows are still in the file.
- Garissa has a single white-maize row. Counties with no rows are thinner still.
- Synthetic weather and loss rules drive the hold-versus-sell comparison. They are not measured on-farm losses.
